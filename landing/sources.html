Sources

Every number and claim on the Vela page, and where it comes from. Vela is a prototype; where a claim rests on our own test, we say so.

40.2% of women over 65 in the EU live alone, and 21.8% of men
Eurostat, EU-27, 2018. Quoted in the Vela product notes.
0 of 156 stranger pairs matched
Vela evaluation, “eval” folder of the repository. Offline test of the app’s face model (Human 3.3.6): 5 people with 3 photos each, 2 photos to enrol, against 4 strangers; 156 stranger pairs, 0 false matches at threshold 0.50.
4 in 5 photos of known people were recognised
Same test: 80% true match over 15 photo pairs (not people), threshold 0.50. The app ships at 0.52; at 0.525 the result is the same (80% true, 0% false). A small sample.
Faces are matched on the phone; your voice is processed on a server
Vela app source: face detection and matching run on the device, saved faces stay in the phone’s storage, and the server has no face handling. Photos from the glasses never leave the device; only spoken words go to the server.
A face is saved only when you introduce the person
Vela app source: a face is enrolled only after the wearer says who the person is and confirms it.
€9 a month, glasses bought once at the optician
Planned price, set in November 2026. Not on sale yet.
